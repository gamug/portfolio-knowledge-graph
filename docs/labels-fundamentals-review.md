# Approved bilingual labels for the fundamentals block (Work item 45, T-811/T-812, T-816)

**Status: approved by Reviewer A on 2026-11-12. 37 rows approved; 21 rows approved after correction.**

This table follows `docs/labels-en-review.md`: Reviewer A approved 37 rows unchanged and 21 with
the corrected comments below. The TBox terms and the 37 metric concepts are frozen in the schema;
`labels.ttl` now carries the metric concepts.

Rules: the 37 metric concepts are the 38 source names minus `value_inputs`; the loader
stops on any valuation row of a masked asset and loads the valuation rows of the other 550
assets. The USD concepts are `ebitda`, `nopat`, `market_capitalization` and
`enterprise_value`; every text carries its generated/rule provenance wording.
`:pricesNotPublished` is the one Asset-level flag: the loader writes it for all 33 masked
assets, including FRC and SBNY, which have no block C filing.
